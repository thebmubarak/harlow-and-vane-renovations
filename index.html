What happens next?

No mystery.
No guessing.
A process.

Old houses hide surprises. Our process is built to find them early — before they become a change order mid-build.

01

Consultation

We visit, talk through your goals, and give you an honest first budget.

02

Survey

Full structural and heritage survey — so we know what's really behind the walls.

03

Design

3D drawings, material selections and a fixed proposal before any work starts.

04

Build

One project manager, daily updates, and a schedule you can actually plan around.

05

Handover

Walkthrough, snagging, deep clean, and a 12-month workmanship guarantee.
